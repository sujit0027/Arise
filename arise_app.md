**App Name** Arise
Routines aur Modes automation ek **Student ke liye game-changer** ho sakta hai, kyunki students ki life me sabse badi fight **Distraction aur Focus** ki hoti hai. 

Arise/Samsung Routine ek student ki help kaise karega, aur ise students ke liye aur zyada behad behtar kaise banaya ja sakta hai, iski poori details niche hain:

---

### **Part 1: Student ko ye kaise help kar sakta hai? (Benefits)**

1. **Strict Study Mode (Distraction-Free Environment):**
   * Students padhai karte waqt easily Instagram, YouTube, ya games ki taraf swipe kar dete hain. Block list feature un apps ko bilkul open nahi hone dega jab tak study timer khatam na ho.
2. **Visual Conditioning (Wallpaper Cues):**
   * Jab mode start hoga, toh visual cue ke roop me ek custom motivational wallpaper ya study schedule lag jayega. Ye humare brain ko signal deta hai ki *"Ab padhai ka time ho chuka hai."* Aur break hone par normal wallpaper lag jayega.
3. **Lock Screen Countdown (Pomodoro Effect):**
   * Lock screen par chalta hua countdown timer student ko screen open karte hi focus me rakhta hai: *"Chalo sirf 15 minutes baaki hain, focus rakhta hoon."*
4. **Bedtime Routine (Achhi Neend):**
   * Students late night screen time ki wajah se neend kharab karte hain. Auto time trigger se raat 11 baje app lock active ho jayega, brightness drop ho jayegi aur screen dim ho jayegi taaki wo phone side me rakh kar so sakein.
5. **No Decision Fatigue (Automatic Triggers):**
   * Padhne ke liye manually app open karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Roz sham ko 6 baje alarm trigger hote hi phone automatically study settings active kar dega.

---

### **Part 2: Students ke liye isme aur kya behtar ho sakta hai? (Future Upgrades)**

Paisa aur time bachane aur study focus ko double karne ke liye hum isme aage jaakar ye features add kar sakte hain:

#### **1. Whitelist Mode (Sabse behtar feature):**
* *Abhi kya hai:* Aap un apps ko choose karte hain jinhe block karna hai.
* *Behtar kya hoga:* **Whitelist Mode**, jisme app sirf un app ko chunoge jisse use karna hai

#### **2. Daily Target on Lockscreen:**
* Lock screen par sirf timer hi nahi, balki unka daily target (e.g., "Math Chapter 3 target today") ya koi energetic quote refresh hota rahe.
